Take Action: Free Dr. Hussam Abu Safiya

Dr. Hussam Abu Safiya, pediatrician and director of Gaza's Kamal Adwan Hospital, has been held by Israel without charge since December 2024. His lawyer reports severe torture and a life-threatening condition. UN experts and the Independent Commission of Inquiry on the OPT have called for his immediate release, but Israel's Supreme Court extended his detention to at least October 2026.
